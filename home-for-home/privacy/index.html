Privacy policy — Home for Home

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Home for Home (com.codonist.homeforhome) is a dots-and-boxes puzzle. You can play every mode without an account. Signing in with Google is optional. It exists so your profile, progress and results can belong to you, and because online matches need an identity.

Data kept on your device

The app saves these items in local storage on the phone:

  • a random player id created on this install
  • the unfinished board, so Continue can restore it
  • the results of finished matches (board size, scores, and win or loss), up to the latest 100
  • your settings: theme (light or dark), sound and vibration, board size, computer difficulty
  • whether the first-time hint has been shown
  • if you signed in: your name, email and picture URL, so the app opens already signed in

This data stays on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it. The developer cannot see it.

If you sign in with Google

Google returns four items for this app:

  • an account id for Home for Home (a Firebase user id)
  • your display name
  • your email address
  • the web address of your profile picture

The app asks only for the email and profile scopes. It cannot read your contacts, Gmail, Drive, Calendar or Photos. Your Google password is never shared with the app, and the sign-in token is handled by Google's Firebase SDK on your device, not written into the game's saved data.

Who processes it

Google LLC handles the sign-in and the account record through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. See Google's privacy policy. Google is the only third party involved. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no other server, and no gameplay data leaves the device today.

Home for Home's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The account data is used only to sign you in, to show your name and picture in the profile screen, and to attach your profile and results to your account. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used to train AI models, and read by no one but the developer.

What the app does not collect

No contacts, Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Photos (only the picture address you already publish on your Google account), location, phone number, advertising id or device identifiers. No ad networks, no trackers, no in-app purchases in this version.

Security

Game data lives in the app's private storage on the phone. The account record lives in Firebase (Google Cloud) and travels over HTTPS. Access tokens are held by the Firebase SDK on the device. No passwords are stored anywhere by the app.

Deleting your data

  • Guest: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Signed in: Profile → Sign out. Your device keeps its own data and you carry on as a guest.
  • Delete the account itself: use the account deletion page or email hamayunaziz.codonist@gmail.com from the same Google address. The account record (id, name, email, picture address) is deleted within 7 days and you get a confirmation. No other copy exists on any server.
  • Deleting the account does not delete the game data on the phone; clear the app's storage or uninstall for that.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask for corrections, ask for deletion, or withdraw consent at any time — signing out withdraws consent for the account; the rest of the game keeps working without it. Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you also have the right to object and to data portability, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Under California law, no personal information is sold or shared, and there is no financial incentive offered for data. Requests: email the address below.

Children

Home for Home is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA and the UK). Signing in needs a Google account, which Google does not offer to younger children. The app does not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has signed in, email the address below and the account will be deleted.

Changes

The date at the top changes whenever this policy does. When online play, leaderboards, ads or in-app purchases arrive, this policy will name every new third party before that version is published, and the in-app release notes will point to the change.
