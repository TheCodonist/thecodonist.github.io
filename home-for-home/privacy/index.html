Privacy policy — Home for Home

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Home for Home (com.codonist.homeforhome) is an offline puzzle. This version does not create an account and does not send gameplay data to a server.

Data stored on your device

The app saves these items in local storage on the phone:

  • a random player id created on this install
  • the unfinished board, so Continue can restore it
  • the results of finished matches (board size, scores, and win or loss), up to the latest 100
  • the theme you chose (light or dark)
  • whether the first-time hint has been shown

This data stays on the device. Uninstalling the app removes it. The developer cannot see it.

Data the app does not collect

This version has no ads, no analytics, no account login, and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
