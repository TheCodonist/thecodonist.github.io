Delete your Home for Home account

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This page is for players of Home for Home (com.codonist.homeforhome) on Google Play who signed in with Google and want that account deleted.

The app needs no account

Signing in is optional. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete — everything stays on your phone. To remove it, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → Home for Home → Storage → Clear data.

Delete the account

Email from the same Google address you signed in with, using the subject Delete my Home for Home account:

hamayunaziz.codonist@gmail.com

If you would rather not email, you can reply to any message from the developer — same result. The request is handled by a person, so nothing else is needed.

What is deleted

  • the account record: account id, display name, email address, profile picture address
  • the sign-in itself, so the app can no longer open signed in as you

Done within 7 days of the request, with a confirmation email. Nothing else about you is held on any server, because the game does not upload gameplay data yet.

What stays

Your progress, finished matches and settings live on your phone only, under the app's own storage. Deleting the account does not touch them — the app drops back to guest mode. To remove that data too, uninstall the app or use Clear data in Android settings.

Coming later

When online matches, levels and leaderboards arrive, those records will live on the server and this page will list them under “What is deleted” before that version is published.
